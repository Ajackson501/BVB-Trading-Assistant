Educational decision-support tool only. The app prioritizes market structure and location, then setup and trigger, and only permits a TRADE state after structure confirmation, a minimum quality score, and estimated reward/risk of at least 2:1. The 20 SMA is calculated automatically. A true daily 200 SMA can be entered manually when the data feed does not provide 200 closes; the app labels its source, never substitutes a shorter average as a fake 200 SMA, and plots a horizontal manual 200-SMA reference line on either chart view when supplied. The chart can switch between the default close-price line and daily OHLC candlesticks. Historical setup behavior remains a small background input.
